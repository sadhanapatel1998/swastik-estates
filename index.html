Property Guide

6 Things Every Property Buyer Must Check

Make confident, informed decisions with these expert checks before finalizing any Delhi property deal.

* General industry best practices. For final legal verifications, consult qualified registry attorneys.

Talk to an Expert
01Verify Chain Documents

Ensure allotment letters, conveyance deeds, mutation and seller identity are fully cross-verified.

02Real Market Comparison

Compare per-square-yard rates and recent transaction registry values in the same block.

03Daylight Site Visits

Inspect in natural daylight to assess ventilation, sunlight, street width, and neighborhood vibe.

04Dues & Utility NOCs

Check BSES electricity, Delhi Jal Board water meters, house tax receipts, and municipal NOCs.

05Total Budget Estimation

Factor stamp duty, transfer charges, interior renovation, and registration expenses beforehand.

06Clear Written Terms

Document payment milestones, earnest money agreements, and handover timelines in clear legal writing.
